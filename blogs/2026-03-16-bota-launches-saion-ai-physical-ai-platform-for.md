As AI reshapes the digital world through cognitive and generative capabilities, a new frontier emerges: Physical AI — an intelligent system that perceives, reasons through, and acts in the physical world. Today, Bota launches SAION AI, the first Physical AI platform for biomanufacturing. SAION AI is not limited to in silico design.
