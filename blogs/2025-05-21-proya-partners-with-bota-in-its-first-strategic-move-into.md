On May 20, 2025, Bota Biosciences (referred to as Bota) and Proya Co., Ltd. (referred to as Proya) held a strategic cooperation signing ceremony at Proya’s Longwu R&D Center in Hangzhou. This milestone marks Proya’s first strategic collaboration with a synthetic biology company.
